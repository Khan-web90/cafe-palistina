WELCOME TO CAFÉ PALESTINA

More than coffee.
A taste of home.

Tucked into the heart of Norwalk, Café Palestina is a small ritual — a place where Arabic coffee meets American mornings, where Palestinian sweets find their way to late-night conversations.

Every cup is brewed with intention. Every plate carries the warmth of a kitchen that has always welcomed guests.

Rooted in Palestine.
Brewed for here.
